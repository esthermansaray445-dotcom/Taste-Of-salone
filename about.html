About Us

Our Story

Taste of Salone was born from a love for great food, our culture and our people. We are a locally owned restaurant in Sierra Leone, dedicated to sharing authentic flavours, warm hospitality and a unique dining experience with our community and visitors.

Our Mission

To celebrate Sierra Leonean cuisine, support local farmers and create a memorable dining experience for everyone.

Our Vision

To be the leading restaurant in Sierra Leone, known for our quality food, excellent service and commitment to our local community.

Our Values

Quality

We serve fresh, high-quality ingredients.

Authenticity

We keep our traditions alive.

Community

We support local farmers and businesses.

Hospitality

You are always welcome here.
